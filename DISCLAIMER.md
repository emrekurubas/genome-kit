The results are not absolute findings and may be wrong or incomplete. In particular:

- **Consumer DNA chips are not clinical tests.** They often misread rare variants, so a reported "disease-causing" variant may not be real. A variant that is present may also be missed or not tested at all.
- **Interpretations change.** They rely on public databases and published research, including ClinVar, PharmCAT and CPIC, the PGS Catalog, 1000 Genomes, AADR, AlphaMissense and REVEL. These sources contain errors, change over time and may not apply equally to every ancestry.
- **Risk estimates are not predictions.** Polygenic scores, risk figures and ancestry estimates are statistical estimates. They do not tell you what will happen to you.
